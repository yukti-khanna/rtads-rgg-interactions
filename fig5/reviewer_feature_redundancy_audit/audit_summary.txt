7. Does compute_pair_features.py reproduce historical OCN and Normalized_OCN definitions exactly? NO.
8. Precisely what differs? Let P count positive residues and N negative residues. Current OCN = -(P_R-N_R)*(P_T-N_T) = P_R*N_T+N_R*P_T-P_R*P_T-N_R*N_T. Portable OCN = P_R*N_T+N_R*P_T, omitting the same-sign subtraction. Their difference is P_R*P_T+N_R*N_T under a common charge convention. Portable normalized OCN also divides by the product of lengths rather than their sum. Raw sequences/positive-negative counts are not in the current pair tables, so the portable numerator was not recomputed; the OCN/product-length numeric test isolates the denominator mismatch using stored OCN. The training/pruning scripts read the historical OCN column rather than computing it. Prediction only adds missing engineered columns, allowing portable definitions to pass through unchanged.
Additional discrepancies: portable lambda_RGG/TAD are sequence means; historical extensive values match per-length Average_lambda/Min_lambda formulas within stored numeric precision (up to 6.38e-10 held out). This supports sum-like scale but does not recover the missing original residue-level implementation. Portable Lambda_Ratio is RGG/TAD without epsilon; historical is TAD/(RGG+1e-6). Portable Hydropathy_Diff is absolute mean-lambda difference; historical is signed SHD_TAD-SHD_RGG. Portable Average_Interaction equals Average_lambda; historical values do not. Other historical base-feature formulas remain explicitly unresolved. See implementation_discrepancies.csv.

9. Which final ten have duplicated/overlapping electrostatic information? OCN, Normalized_OCN and charge_TAD all directly contain bulk charge. None is a pairwise exact duplicate of another in these data. OCN and Normalized_OCN have strongly overlapping information; charge_TAD is a constituent of the product but is not determined by OCN alone. Their joint deterministic dependencies should guide grouped ablation; do not infer independent biological mechanisms from separate importances.

10. Which final features are distinct from bulk net-charge magnitude? TAD_length is length. lambda_RGG and lambda_TAD describe hydropathy/stickiness on a historical extensive scale; Lambda_Ratio is deterministically derived from them. SHD_TAD and Average_SHD are hydropathy-decoration descriptors (historical precise formula unresolved). These describe information beyond a net-charge product, although correlations with charge/length can remain. Average_Interaction is a distinct stored column, but its original formula is missing, so it cannot be certified electrostatics-free. Strong correlations do not establish biological equivalence.

NUMERICAL AUDIT COVERAGE
28 stored sequence-derived columns in each panel; 26 original pruning candidates plus Average_sigma and Average_Aromatics. Outcomes, predictions, names/IDs and the Unnamed index were excluded explicitly. All 45 selected-ten pairs are in selected10_correlations.csv; all 378 candidate pairs per panel are in all_pair_correlations.csv and duplicate_and_affine_checks.csv. Pearson/Spearman matrices retain constant columns as undefined (NaN), shown gray in figures. High-correlation output is the union of |r|>=0.90 OR |rho|>=0.90, without rounding before thresholding. Stats include population and sample variance, unique values, missing and infinite counts; selected10 membership provides the separate subset audit. Exact duplicate pairs: 0. Nonconstant affine-equivalent pairs within numerical tolerance: 0. Constant Average_Aromatics is zero throughout both panels. Constant columns were not treated as informative affine-equivalent descriptors.

Rounding and methods: pandas read_csv(float_precision='round_trip'), float64 arithmetic; n_exact uses strict ==, never an approximate comparison. Separate allclose consistency uses atol=rtol=1e-12 and is explicitly labeled. This tolerance distinguishes algebraic identities perturbed by CSV serialization from strong correlations; it does not prove an unknown generating formula. Pearson uses pairwise finite observations (all audited feature values are finite here); Spearman uses average ranks for ties. Affine screening estimates slope/intercept by centered least squares and tests every row at the same tolerance; it is descriptive numerical evidence, not mathematical proof. Stored precision varies by dataset AND column. Engineered held-out features are exactly reproducible, while several held-out base-feature relationships differ by up to 1.65e-9 because their upstream columns are rounded. The exact_relationships.csv conclusion additionally flags max error <= 1e-8 as consistent with rounded upstream inputs, NOT exact and NOT a pass of the tighter 1e-12 test. This secondary diagnostic is not source-level proof of the historical formula. Training engineered formulas have residual errors at about 1e-14 or less.
