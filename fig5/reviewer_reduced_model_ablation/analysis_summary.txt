2. Charge patterning beyond OCN: M2 changes held-out R2 by +0.011667, RMSE by -0.005065, and MAE by -0.003184. Matrix-bootstrap support using joint criteria (ΔR2 CI entirely above zero and ΔRMSE CI entirely below zero): True. SCD remains an electrostatic patterning descriptor.

7. Conservative classification: almost entirely bulk electrostatics within the resolution of this ablation. This classification uses a transparent descriptive threshold of ΔR2 >= 0.10 for “substantial”; that threshold was not supplied by the reviewer and is not a formal hypothesis test. The defensible prose should emphasize the observed point differences and two-axis intervals. The analysis can test whether the current predictor is “just recovering charge complementarity,” but it cannot identify mechanisms or make electrostatic dominance novel.

Matrix-aware uncertainty
For each of 2,000 replicates (seed 20260911), 78 RGG IDs were sampled with replacement and 23 TAD IDs were independently sampled with replacement. The 78x23 indexed Cartesian cells were then flattened. Repeated sampled IDs were retained with full multiplicity through repeated row/column indices; nothing was deduplicated. Every model was evaluated on the same cells within a replicate, enabling paired differences from M1. Percentile 2.5th and 97.5th quantiles form the reported 95% intervals. This two-axis cluster bootstrap reflects dependence induced by both shared RGG and shared TAD partners. It does not correct for model-training uncertainty, sequence homology, or the deliberate composition of the 78x23 panel. No naive pair-level bootstrap was used.

Interpretive limits
Training-set scores are apparent fit scores, not cross-validated estimates. The ensemble is fit once per seed and feature set according to the pre-specified design; confidence intervals resample only held-out matrix axes. MLP optimization is stochastic but fixed by seeds. Residual correlations are descriptive diagnostics and do not imply causality. The historical SCD/SHD formulas remain limited by Task 1 provenance; this task uses stored audited values. Derived mean lambdas are transparent ratios and leave original columns unchanged.
