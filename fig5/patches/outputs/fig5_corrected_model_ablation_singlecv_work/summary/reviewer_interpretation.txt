Reviewer-facing interpretation:
Bulk electrostatic complementarity remains the dominant signal. Adding charge_TAD provides a small increment over OCN alone. The corrected non-electrostatic-only M2 shows that global hydropathy/AH, patterning, and length variables have predictive information without explicit charge-product descriptors. The non-redundant combined M3 improves over M1 and performs close to the historical ORIGINAL10 reference while avoiding Normalized_OCN as a duplicate OCN representation. Adding the frozen local patch descriptor opp_charge_std in M4 modestly improves R2, RMSE, Pearson, and Spearman versus M3, while MAE is not uniformly improved. Thus local electrostatic heterogeneity should be described as a modest additional contribution beyond the non-redundant global model, not as a replacement for the dominant OCN effect or a new sequence grammar.
