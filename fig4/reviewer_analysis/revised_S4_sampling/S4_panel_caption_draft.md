# S4 Panel Caption Draft

S4A. Pilot-stage B22 versus charge complementarity for the 25-pair pilot set (n=25). This panel documents an early feature relationship considered during sampling design. It does not represent an independent validation test.

S4B. Pilot-stage B22 versus mean pair NCPR for the 25-pair pilot set (n=25). This panel documents charge-composition space explored during dataset construction. It does not establish unbiased discovery performance.

S4C. Pilot-stage B22 versus average lambda for the 25-pair pilot set (n=25). This panel documents the hydropathy/stickiness region considered during the early sampling design. It does not test independent generalisation.

S4D. Early simulated B22 versus net-charge product, equivalent to the implemented OCN value (-Q_RGG x Q_TAD), for the initial simulation set (n=595). This relationship motivated use of net-charge product as a later-stage sampling coordinate. It does not imply that the final 917 pairs were randomly selected or sampled by known B22.

S4E. Empirical net-charge-product distributions for the complete candidate landscape (n=231,840), feature-guided simulation panel (n=917), and independent sequence-exclusive simulation set (n=1,794). The feature-guided panel is modestly shifted relative to the full candidate space (SMD=0.216), whereas the independent set is closer to the full-space distribution (SMD=-0.044). This panel describes sampling coverage, not a biological validation result.

S4F. Empirical total-pair-length distributions for the complete candidate landscape, feature-guided simulation panel, and independent sequence-exclusive simulation set. The final 917-pair panel is modestly shifted relative to the full candidate landscape (SMD=0.165). This panel describes sampling composition, not independent validation.

S4G. Empirical average-lambda distributions for the complete candidate landscape, feature-guided simulation panel, and independent sequence-exclusive simulation set. The final 917-pair panel is close to the full candidate landscape for average lambda (SMD=-0.072). This panel describes feature-space coverage, not independent validation.

S4H. Reconstructed net-charge-product bin coverage for the complete candidate landscape and final 917-pair feature-guided simulation panel. Historical per-round bin manifests were not found, so bin counts are reconstructed from the retained final pair list and full candidate landscape. The panel shows coverage across the OCN range and should not be interpreted as per-round historical selection counts.
