Figure 4. Simulated R-TAD-RGG interaction landscape and sequence features. (A) B22 interaction landscape for the original 917 simulated R-TAD-RGG pairs. This panel preserves the unchanged historical analysis and corrected B22 distribution. (B) Representative RDFs for the historical strong/interacting and weak/non-interacting example pairs. Lines show the mean pH 7.0 RDF across five historical replicas (`job_0`-`job_4`), and shaded bands show between-replica SD. (C) Corrected sequence-oriented residue-class attractive interaction-energy enrichment, shown as log2(observed / expected), with expected values from attractive-energy matrix marginals. Rows are RGG residue classes and columns are R-TAD residue classes. The color scale is clipped to +/-2 for display, while finite cell annotations show the actual values. Dash indicates no net attractive contribution. (D) Relationship between net-charge product, the quantity implemented previously as OCN, and log10(-B22) in the feature-guided 917-pair simulation panel and in the independent sequence-exclusive 1,794-pair set. The independent set tests generalization outside the feature-guided sampling design. For net-charge product, the 917 panel has Pearson r=0.875 and Spearman rho=0.956; the independent set has Pearson r=0.924 and Spearman rho=0.923.
