# Supplementary Figure S4 Legend Draft

Supplementary Figure S4. Sampling provenance and independent validation for Figure 4. (S4A-S4D) Historical feature relationships examined during the iterative sampling strategy: charge complementarity, mean pair NCPR, average λ, and the early relationship between B22 and negative net-charge product (−NCP). These panels document sampling-design provenance and are not independent validation. (S4E-S4G) Feature distributions for the complete 231,840-pair candidate landscape, the final feature-guided 917-pair simulation panel, and the independent 1,794-pair sequence-exclusive set. (S4H) Reconstructed −NCP-bin coverage for the final 917-pair panel. Historical per-round bin manifests were not recovered, so counts are reconstructed from the retained final pair list and full candidate landscape. (S4I) Independent validation of the −NCP-B22 relationship in the sequence-exclusive set made from 23 TADs and 78 RGGs, yielding the complete 1,794-pair cross-product with no TAD or RGG sequence overlap with the original 917-pair panel. The independent set has Pearson r=0.924 and Spearman ρ=0.923, testing generalization outside the feature-guided sampling design.
