### S4J

Recovery of the 627 unique RG/RGG regions represented in the final 917 CALVADOS pairs following one-factor perturbation of the compact-linker, bridge-linker, or flank-extension thresholds used to define compact RG regions. Recovery is defined primarily by any coordinate overlap with a region identified under the perturbed definition; baseline parameters were compact linker = 7 aa, bridge linker = 15 aa, and flank extension = 5 aa. Observed values are exported in `S4J_region_recovery_plot_data.csv`; altered boundaries are retained when overlap remains.

### S4K

Fraction of the final 917 CALVADOS pairs whose RG/RGG region remained represented under the same one-factor threshold perturbations. Overlapping regions with shifted boundaries were considered retained, so boundary changes caused by altered flank extension were not classified as tract loss. Exact-coordinate and lost counts are provided in `S4K_pair_recovery_plot_data.csv`.
