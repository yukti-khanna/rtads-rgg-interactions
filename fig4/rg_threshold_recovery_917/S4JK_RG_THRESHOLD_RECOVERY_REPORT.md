# S4J–S4K RG-threshold recovery report

This report is generated from the completed one-factor recovery tables. The runnable plotting script writes the two plotting-data CSVs and the requested PDF/600-dpi PNG outputs.

Verified denominators: 627 unique downstream RG/RGG regions and 917 final simulation pairs. Baseline `(7,15,5)` is required to recover 627/627 regions and 917/917 pairs.

For every perturbation, the exported CSVs report parameter values, any-overlap counts/fractions, exact-coordinate counts/fractions, and lost counts. Any-overlap is the primary biological retention measure; altered boundaries with overlap are not loss.

Interpretation should be taken directly from the observed exported values: compare the minimum any-overlap recovery within each family, identify the perturbation at that minimum, and distinguish coordinate shifts from tract loss. No claim of random sampling or independent validation is made.
