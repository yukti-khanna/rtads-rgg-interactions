B22 comparison uses the established 7.5 nm corr1 corrected radial sum without dr; no physical units are assigned to this sum. Predictions are already log10(-B22); simulation logs follow replica averaging. Kd is converted from explicitly specified units; pKd=6-log10(Kd in uM). pKd error is first-order propagation of the reported Kd error, preserving its original uncertainty interpretation. Pairs share constructs; permutation tests and row-bootstrap intervals are exploratory. LB denotes the reported low-binding category and NB the reported no-binding category; neither implies a fitted Kd. Untested pairs are never assigned NB. Prediction agreement and shifts do not establish accuracy.

Every source and its SHA256 is recorded in source_selection.json. Experimental duplicate rows are preserved; unselected conflicts have no pair-level Kd. Paper prediction/pKd limits use only the current displayed predictor values. Shared limits occur only in old/new diagnostic comparisons. Simulated values are retained even when comparison provenance is unresolved. Accuracy comparisons bind the existing audit to the selected simulation values and exact sequences. No RDF, B22 or model recovery is rerun by this entrypoint. Anonymous feature rows require an explicit feature_row map and matching manifest hashes. Additional archived descriptors remain separately named, including their original lengths and matching status.
