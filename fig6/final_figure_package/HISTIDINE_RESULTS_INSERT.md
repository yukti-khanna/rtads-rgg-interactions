The NMR titrations were performed at pH 6.5, whereas the reference simulations used pH 7.0. We therefore examined whether adjusting histidine protonation to the experimental pH altered the simulated interaction landscape. Across 390 matched pairs, scores at the two pH values remained closely correlated (Pearson r = 0.98; Spearman ρ = 0.96), with a mean change of approximately 0.001 and an RMSE of 0.061 in log₁₀(−B₂₂) (Fig. S10A). The frozen predictor also retained agreement with the pH 6.5 simulations (R² = 0.95; RMSE = 0.07; Pearson r = 0.98; Fig. S10B). These controls support the robustness of the broad computational ranking to the modelled histidine-protonation adjustment, although they do not establish that individual experimental affinities are insensitive to pH.
