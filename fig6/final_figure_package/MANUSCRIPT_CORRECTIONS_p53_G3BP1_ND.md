# Corrections after curator confirmation: p53–G3BP1 is ND

This supersedes prior text reporting p53–G3BP1 as a fitted interaction. Its former Kd (278.835 ± 88.146 µM) is invalid for the current curated dataset and must not be reported. The pair remains in outcome analyses as ND, with no Kd, pKd or bound fraction. Historical audit records retain the previous entry to document the correction.

## Current Results and legend values

The 52 WT pairs comprise 13 Kd fits, nine LB responses and 30 ND responses. Ten shuffle controls remain separate; measured shuffle intensity ratios remain in the exploratory intensity analysis.

Predicted scores differed among outcome groups (permutation Kruskal–Wallis p = 0.00005). Kd-fit scores exceeded both ND and LB scores (Holm-adjusted p = 0.00015 for each comparison). LB and ND were not significantly separated (p = 0.9602).

Predicted AUROC for Kd fit versus ND was 0.882 (95% grouped-bootstrap intervals: 0.764–0.993 by rAHD parent; 0.694–0.965 by RG/RGG partner), compared with 0.933 for simulation-derived scores. Predicted AUROC was 0.723 for Kd fit plus LB versus ND and 0.493 for LB versus ND. The exploratory cutoff remained 3.716, with balanced accuracy 0.833 within the panel, 0.756 when withholding rAHD parents and 0.773 when withholding RG/RGG partners.

Among the 13 fitted WT interactions, predicted scores correlated with pKd (Spearman rho = 0.692; permutation p = 0.01205). Rank concordance was 0.769 (95% grouped intervals: 0.556–0.906 by rAHD parent; 0.500–0.864 by RG/RGG partner). Simulation-derived scores had Spearman rho = 0.500 (permutation p = 0.08545). These comparisons support coarse rank agreement, not precise affinity prediction.

## Reviewer-response corrections

For p53, THOC4-N yielded a fitted Kd of 146 ± 11 µM, whereas G3BP1, TAF15 and THOC4-C were classified as ND. FOXM1–THOC4-N retained its fitted Kd of 282 ± 90 µM.

The protein-level p53–G3BP1 PPI record remains in the source network, but the tested fragments showed no detectable interaction under the NMR conditions. Among the mapped TP53–G3BP1, TP53–THOC4 and LEF1–THOC4 associations, p53–THOC4-N yielded a fitted Kd. Protein-level association evidence must not be equated with binding between the isolated fragments.

Finally, estimated bound fractions at the highest measured ligand concentrations are now available for all 13 fitted WT interactions, ranging from 35.1% to 88.0% (Supplementary Table S11). Lower fractions are expected for higher Kd values at comparable concentrations, but these estimates depend on the fitted Kd and do not independently validate the fits. Several titrations did not approach saturation, limiting affinity precision. No bound fractions are assigned to LB or ND responses; ND does not exclude weaker binding beyond the tested concentration range.
